What the press ordered

Street-food favorites, from the Wisconsin State Journal’s first visit

Lad na mee khob

Crispy egg noodles, Chinese broccoli, and a sweet, thick soy–oyster sauce poured over the top. One of the dishes found only at Moom Thai.

Puffy fried Thai omelet over rice

A street classic fried puffy, served over rice. Add a protein — shrimp is the way many regulars take it. More about the omelet →

Moo ma now

Sliced marinated pork with steamed Chinese broccoli and a green chili–garlic dipping sauce on the side.

Pad Thai

One of Monsoon Siam’s most popular plates — the only one of them to move to the South Side. More about the pad Thai →

Massaman curry and kao soi stay on the Monsoon Siam menu for now. Dishes and availability can shift during soft opening — ask at the counter for what’s cooking today.
