The corner story

“I bring a taste of Thai street food”

“I bring a taste of Thai street food, and I’m cooking on my own, too, so everything is made from scratch and quick grab-and-go.”

— Dutdao “Pooh” Wonglaka, owner and cook, to the Wisconsin State Journal

Pooh opened Monsoon Siam on Atwood Avenue in 2014. Moom Thai — her restaurant alone, on its own corner, in the old Asian Sweet Bakery space — is the South Side version: the street plates, a shorter menu, and the same hands on the wok. Read more about the corner →
