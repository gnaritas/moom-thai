About

Her corner, her wok, her rules

Moom Thai is the South Side restaurant that Dutdao “Pooh” Wonglaka built for one reason: the corner opened, and she said yes.

“Okay, I’ll do one more here”

The space at 1017 S Park St — 900 square feet on the corner of South Park and Lakeside — was Asian Sweet Bakery, Madison’s first Chinese bakery, for twelve years. It closed in August. By late October, Pooh had rearranged the tables, put in kitchen equipment built for a Thai kitchen, and started cooking.

“It’s a good opportunity that this building is open, so I just said, ‘Okay, I’ll do one more here.’”

— Pooh Wonglaka, to the Wisconsin State Journal

The name is the place

“Moom” is a Thai word meaning “corner,” and the restaurant sits on one: South Park and Lakeside. That is the whole naming story, told by Pooh herself.

Sister, not twin

Pooh also owns Monsoon Siam on Atwood Avenue, which she opened in 2014. Moom Thai is hers alone — a different name, a shorter menu built for grab-and-go, and some street plates the Atwood menu does not carry. Her gift shop, Siam Boutique, is right next door.

She cooks everything at Moom Thai herself, with two employees on the counter side. During soft opening she is keeping promotion quiet on purpose: “I want them to come try,” she told the State Journal.
