Privacy

What this site does with your information

The contact form

The form asks for a name and a message, and optionally a phone number and an email address. Those details go to Moom Thai so the counter can answer you — nothing else. Notes are kept only as long as it takes to reply and settle the question.

No trackers, no cookies

This site sets no cookies and loads no advertising or analytics scripts. A tiny invisible image on the shop’s email messages counts views only as a whole — no personal profiles are built.

The map on the Visit and Contact pages

The location map is loaded from Google Maps, so Google sees the page request that paints it. The shop stores no map data about you.

Your call

Calling the shop number is ordinary phone traffic between you and Moom Thai.

Questions

Use the contact form and this site’s team will pass it to the shop.
