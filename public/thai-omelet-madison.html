The signature plate

Puffy fried Thai omelet,
over rice

Eggs fried puffy and golden, served over rice, with a protein to add — shrimp is the counter’s go-to. It was on the first table the Wisconsin State Journal visited, and it is exactly what Thai street food looks like.

Made to order, every time

Nothing sits in a warmer at Moom Thai. Tell the counter iPad what you want — omelet plain or with shrimp — and it hits the hot wok the moment your order lands. Grab a box or take one of the two long tables.

The omelet is one of the street plates that makes Moom Thai different from sister restaurant Monsoon Siam: a shorter menu, built around the quick, egg-and-wok side of Thai street cooking.

Where can I get a fried Thai omelet in Madison?

At Moom Thai, 1017 S Park St, Madison, WI 53715 — the corner of South Park and Lakeside. Open Tuesday–Sunday, noon to 7; closed Monday.

Can I get the omelet to go?

Yes — the whole shop is built for grab-and-go. Two long tables seat eight each if you would rather sit a while.

Can I add protein?

Yes. Add a protein such as shrimp to the omelet at the counter.

Is Moom Thai the same restaurant as Monsoon Siam?

Different restaurant, same owner. Pooh Wonglaka opened Monsoon Siam on Atwood Avenue in 2014; Moom Thai is hers alone and carries street plates the Atwood menu does not.
