Same favorite, shorter walk

If you already know the Atwood kitchen, this is the plate you know, cooked to order on the corner of South Park and Lakeside. If you are new to it: wok-fried noodles, sweet-tangy sauce, the works — boxed for the road or eaten at one of the long tables.

South Side regulars who used to drive across town for it no longer have to. In the State Journal’s telling, the reaction has been warm: “They’re so excited that we’re here, too.”

Where can I get pad Thai on Madison’s South Side?

At Moom Thai, 1017 S Park St, Madison, WI 53715 — the corner of South Park and Lakeside. Open Tuesday–Sunday, noon to 7; closed Monday.

Do Monsoon Siam and Moom Thai share a menu?

No. Moom Thai’s menu is about half the size of Monsoon Siam’s. Pad Thai is the one Atwood favorite served at both; massaman curry and kao soi stay at Monsoon Siam.

Is it grab-and-go or sit-down?

Built for grab-and-go, with two long tables that seat eight each if you want to eat in.

Are there other dishes only at Moom Thai?

Yes — street-food plates like lad na mee khob (crispy egg noodles with Chinese broccoli and a sweet soy-oyster sauce) and the puffy fried Thai omelet over rice.
